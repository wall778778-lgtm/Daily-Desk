Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens when you use Daily Desk. The short version: your planning entries are stored locally in your browser and are not sent to a Daily Desk server.

Information stored locally

When you use the dashboard, your focus, tasks, habit check-ins, and notes are stored in your browser's local storage so they remain available when you return. Clearing your browser data or using a different device may remove or change this information.

Analytics and advertising

Before advertising or analytics services are added, this page will be updated with the relevant provider names and links to their policies. Third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, prevent fraud, or show relevant advertisements. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Children's privacy

Daily Desk is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the service changes. The date at the top will show when it was last revised.
