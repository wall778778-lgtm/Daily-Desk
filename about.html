About the tool

A little less noise. A little more intention.

Daily Desk is a free browser-based planning tool for people who want a simple place to decide what matters today. It brings a short task list, small wellbeing check-ins, notes, and a reflective prompt into one calm screen.

Designed for real days

This is intentionally not a complicated productivity system. You do not need an account, a streak, or a perfect routine. The tool is here to help you choose one useful focus and make the next step visible.

Your entries stay yours

Daily Desk stores your focus, tasks, habits, and notes in your own browser using local storage. The entries are not sent to a Daily Desk server. See the privacy page for more detail.
